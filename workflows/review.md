# Workflow: brand review

## Inputs

- the artifact or implemented surface;
- its brief and acceptance criteria;
- applicable brand rules;
- target sizes, backgrounds, states, and locale.

## Review dimensions

Score each from 1 (fails materially) to 5 (fully resolved):

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | Does it solve the stated customer and business job? |
| Identity | Does it feel like Sve Za Pecanje without leaning on the logo? |
| Clarity | Is hierarchy immediate and specialist meaning correct? |
| Consistency | Does it reuse established geometry, tokens, and language? |
| Accessibility | Does it work with contrast, focus, semantics, and at target sizes? |
| Production | Is the asset/code clean, reusable, responsive, and maintainable? |

## Findings

Use P0–P3. Each finding must pair an observable fact with impact and a concrete
change. “Make it cleaner” is not actionable.

## Verdict

End with exactly one of `approve`, `approve_with_notes`, `revise`, or `reject`,
plus the shortest evidence-based rationale that supports it.
