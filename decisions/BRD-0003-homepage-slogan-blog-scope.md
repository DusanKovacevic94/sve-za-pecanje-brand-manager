# BRD-0003 — Homepage slogan and general blog scope

- **Date:** 2026-09-07
- **Status:** approved
- **Owner / Approver:** Project owner, by explicit instruction in the current request
- **Related brief:** `work/briefs/2026-09-07-home-blog-copy.md`

## Context

The owner specified the main slogan and clarified that the blog is not advice-only.
This records that human decision; it does not independently approve editorial content.

## Decision

Use `Sve za pecanje na jednom mestu.` as the main website slogan, with homepage
supporting text `Pretraži štapove, mašinice, varalice i drugu opremu.`
Do not frame the entire blog as `Saveti za ribolovce` or practical equipment guides.
Individual articles may still be advice or guides when their actual content warrants it.

## Rationale and consequences

The slogan matches the domain and the owner's preferred positioning. General blog
framing accommodates broader editorial content without inventing new sections.
This updates website copy only, not the protected wordmark or logo family.

## Alternatives considered

Keeping the prior slogan or advice-only framing conflicts with the owner's request.
