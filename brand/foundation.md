# Brand foundation

## Purpose

Help people in Serbia find, evaluate, buy, and sell fishing equipment with less
friction and more confidence.

## Promise

Sve Za Pecanje makes a specialist marketplace feel clear, useful, and safe—
whether someone knows the exact reel model they need or is buying their first
rod.

## Character

- **Practical:** information and actions beat decoration.
- **Calm:** hierarchy is confident, never loud or frantic.
- **Knowledgeable:** use the vocabulary anglers expect without excluding
  beginners.
- **Trustworthy:** explain status, risk, price, and next steps plainly.
- **Outdoors-ready:** layouts stay legible on mobile, in glare, and with one
  hand.

## Visual principles

1. **Like well-made equipment.** Components feel clear, durable, and precise.
2. **Fishing, not scenery.** Use essential equipment silhouettes and the
   hook/ripple motif, not decorative landscapes or stock-photo clichés.
3. **Information earns emphasis.** Price, condition, location, trust, and the
   primary action outrank decoration.
4. **One system at every size.** The same geometry and hierarchy should survive
   from a 14 px icon to a campaign panel.
5. **Warm restraint.** Cream and orange keep Pine from feeling institutional;
   neither should weaken readability.

## Core visual tokens

| Token | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pine | `#173F37` | Primary actions, headings, navigation, dark surfaces |
| Orange | `#EE9835` | Small emphasis, featured states, ripple motif |
| Cream | `#F7F6F1` | Page background and quiet surfaces |
| Ink | `#16201D` | Body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, controls, inverse contrast |

Manrope is the product typeface. Use compact, strong, sentence-case headings.
Uppercase is limited to short eyebrows/navigation groups with deliberate
tracking.

Controls and cards use 12–16 px radii. Pills are for compact metadata and
status—not generic containers. Borders create separation; shadows are soft and
low contrast. Functional hover movement never exceeds 2 px.
