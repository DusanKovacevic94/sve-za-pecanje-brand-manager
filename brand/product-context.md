# Product context

## Primary audiences

- experienced anglers searching by category, technique, brand, and technical
  attributes;
- occasional anglers who need plain language and guided filtering;
- private sellers creating trustworthy listings from a phone;
- specialist shops managing inventory and customer inquiries;
- moderators protecting marketplace quality and safety.

## High-value customer jobs

1. Find compatible equipment quickly.
2. Judge condition, legitimacy, price, and seller trust.
3. Contact a seller without exposing unnecessary personal information.
4. Publish a complete ad with good photos and category-specific details.
5. Return to saved searches, favorites, messages, and active listings.

## Brand-critical product moments

- first visit and search;
- category selection and filters;
- listing cards and listing detail;
- sign-up/login before posting an ad;
- listing creation, draft recovery, and moderation status;
- safety guidance, reporting, messages, and seller trust;
- empty, error, loading, success, and destructive states.

Brand work must not hide marketplace facts, weaken discoverability, or trade
accessibility for visual novelty.
