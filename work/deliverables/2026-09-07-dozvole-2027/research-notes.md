   - Lists the 2027 recreational-permit pricing regulation in Gazette 82/2026 dated 4 September 2026. This is an index, not the full regulation or direct official publication.
4. Vode Vojvodine, original operator announcement, 15 January 2026: https://www.vodevojvodine.com/2026/01/15/pocela-prodaja-ribolovnih-dozvola-za-2026-godinu/
