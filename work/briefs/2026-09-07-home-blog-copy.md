# Creative brief — Homepage slogan and general blog framing

- **Date:** 2026-09-07
- **Owner:** Sve Za Pecanje
- **Approver:** Project owner (explicit wording in the current request)
- **Status:** complete — see `work/reviews/2026-09-07-home-blog-copy.md`

## Request and audience

Update first-visit marketplace copy and remove the assumption that the blog only
publishes advice. Visitors should understand the marketplace and browse articles
without a narrower editorial promise than the owner intends.

## Surface and required content

- Homepage H1: `Sve za pecanje na jednom mestu.`
- Homepage supporting text: `Pretraži štapove, mašinice, varalice i drugu opremu.`
- Blog index: a single `Blog` title, no advice-only eyebrow or subtitle.
- Article/preview eyebrow: `Blog`. Empty state and index SEO/social metadata
  must not promise advice or practical guides exclusively.

## Constraints and acceptance

Reuse existing components, tokens and logo exports. Preserve Serbian Latin,
semantic headings, search/post actions, article excerpts, and responsive behavior.
Verify homepage at 320/390/1440px and blog index/articles at 320/1280px; review the
intentional homepage baseline change before gated refresh. No CMS content edits,
new content categories, commits, publication or deployment are authorized.

## Assumptions

`Blog` is the neutral index title. Keep a broad metadata-only description for
search/social previews; do not substitute another visible subtitle. Copy is
owner-specified, so no alternative visual directions or new assets are needed.
